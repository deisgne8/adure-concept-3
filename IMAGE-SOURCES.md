Enhancement preserved each property's recognizable architecture while improving resolution, perspective, exposure, color balance, sky detail, and compression noise. The generated master PNGs remain in the Codex generated-images directory; the website uses optimized WebP copies.

## Enhancement prompt set

All five photographs were processed with the built-in `image_gen` editor. Each prompt locked the original property identity, façade geometry, window and floor layout, and permanent context; requested perspective correction, highlight and shadow recovery, realistic UAE daylight, cleaner sky and glass, sharper natural texture, and compression-noise removal; and prohibited new architecture, signage, people, logos, text, or landscaping. Sheikha Maitha used a landscape website-banner composition. Al Manhal Tower, Al Mushrif Villas, Jasmine Tower, and Al Salam Tower retained portrait architectural framing.
