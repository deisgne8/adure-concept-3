Transforming The Region’s Real Estate Landscape

Abu Dhabi United Real Estate - ADURE - is an integrated UAE real estate company operating across Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Al Ain.

Our foundations are in property management, supported by expertise across leasing and operations, facility management, and financial and legal management. Today, that experience also extends to buying and selling, allowing us to support owners, investors, buyers and tenants through more of their real estate journey.

Across every relationship, our focus is the same: protect the quality of the asset, strengthen its performance and create an experience people can trust.
