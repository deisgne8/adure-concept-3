Transforming The Region’s Property Landscape

ADURE is an integrated UAE real estate company operating across Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Al Ain.

Our expertise spans the full real estate journey, from leasing and operations to facility management, financial and legal management, buying and selling. This integrated experience allows us to support owners, investors, buyers and residents with a more holistic and connected approach to real estate.

Across every relationship, our focus is the same: protect the quality of the asset, strengthen its performance and create an experience people can trust.

Our Story

2002

Over Two Decades Of Excellence

Since 2002, ADURE has built its real estate experience around the professional management of properties and the day-to-day operations that protect their performance.

That foundation now brings together leasing and operations, facility management, and financial and legal management, alongside buying and selling - allowing ADURE to support more of the real estate journey through one company.

Across Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Al Ain, we have grown around one consistent idea: understand the asset and create value across its journey through the way it is managed, occupied and positioned.
